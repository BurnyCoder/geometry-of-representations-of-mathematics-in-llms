Geometry of mathematics in LLMs

Do LLMs organize mathematical concepts geometrically, the way they place days of the week on a circle (Engels et al. 2024)? Each view shows the trained model next to its random-init twin: the same architecture and tokenizer with no learning. Geometry that also appears in the twin comes from word form, not knowledge. Code, preregistration and full statistics: GitHub.

1 · Map of mathematical fields

2D metric MDS of field-centroid distances. Left: arXiv cross-listing distance (ground truth). Middle: trained model. Right: random-init twin. Blue = pure, orange = applied.

2 · Concept cloud

PCA-3D of individual concepts (primary, confirmatory split), coloured by field. Hover to see a concept.

PCA-3D at the pre-registered layer. Lines join items in ground-truth order. Circular families are closed.

4 · Group-like axiom lattice

Each arrow adds one axiom: associative · identity · divisible · commutative. If an axiom is a consistent direction, its arrows are parallel.
