# Sources for the scientific audit

**26 November 2026.** The [independent review](reviews/2026-11-26_scientific_audit.md) compares the following methods with the implementation and the saved observations.

| Question | What the source establishes | How it was used here |
|---|---|---|
| What does distance-matrix agreement measure? | RSA compares representational dissimilarities. The population being generalized to determines which factors must be resampled. [Kriegeskorte et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008) · [Schütt et al., inference on conditions and subjects](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10446828/) | The centroid test shuffles whole field labels. The paired interval quantifies variation from concept resampling inside fixed fields and checkpoints. [Implementation](../src/mathgeom/verification/fields.py) |
| Which inputs can enter a familywise correction? | Holm orders p-values and successively compares them to thresholds for the hypotheses still under consideration. A bootstrap hypothesis test must account for the null distribution. Randomly sampled permutation tests include the observed arrangement. [Holm](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4615733) · [Hall & Wilson](https://doi.org/10.2307/2532163) · [Phipson & Smyth](https://doi.org/10.2202/1544-6115.1585) | Replay the published family, reserve uncalibrated slots in the sensitivity calculation and report the protocol’s CI decision. [Independent implementation](../src/mathgeom/verification/records.py) |
| What does the calendar precedent show? | Engels et al. examine circular features in specific representations, use projections and intervene in calendar tasks. Those experiments differ from a full-space template comparison of isolated concept names. [Engels et al., §§4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14860v3) | Keep the failed full-space controls in the angle verdict. Interpret projection searches under their own post-hoc criteria. [Frozen rule](../docs/prereg.md) |
| When is an axiom family a lattice? | A pair needs a least upper bound and a greatest lower bound in the poset. [Gotti, Lecture 37, Definition 1](https://math.mit.edu/~fgotti/docs/Courses/C.%20Combinatorial%20Analysis/37.%20Intro%20to%20Lattices/Intro%20to%20Lattices.pdf) | Enumerate minimal upper bounds in the actual set of profiles. Offsets require only the directed one-bit pairs. [Configuration](../configs/families.yaml) |
| What counts as a matching mathematical term? | A categorical colimit is defined by a universal property. Mathlib’s metric gluing source gives a construction for a specific system of metric spaces and isometries. [Stacks, §4.14](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/002D) · [Mathlib source](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Topology/MetricSpace/Gluing.html) | Compare each glossary sense with the pinned declaration. Exclude mismatched senses and scopes, record every decision and report coverage. [Crosswalk](../configs/mathlib_review.tsv) |
