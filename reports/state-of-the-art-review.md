# Related representations and scope of comparison

**26 November 2026.** The study compares geometric measurements on a fixed glossary and fixed concept families. Its novelty claim concerns that combination of measurements. The reviewed literature does not support a priority claim for mathematical concept geometry in general.

- Berlioz extracted mathematical terms and definitions from arXiv and investigated dependency and entailment using standard and hyperbolic embeddings. This directly precedes the use of learned vectors to study relations among mathematical terms. [ArGoT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.02801).
- Engels and colleagues identified circular calendar features and tested their causal use on specific tasks. Kantamneni and Tegmark studied numerical representations in addition. Task, checkpoint, layer, representation and readout all matter when comparing those results with the present angle and calendar measurements. [Calendar features](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14860v3) · [Numerical addition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00873).
- Lexical and vector-offset controls are necessary to interpret analogies. Linzen quantified contributions of neighbourhood structure to analogy completion. The study’s spelling and nonce controls address related alternatives in these particular names. [Linzen](https://aclanthology.org/W16-2503/) · [Saved controls](../results/mvp/lexical.json).
- The Qwen2.5-Math report documents its initialization and math corpus. A single pair of released checkpoints can bound the observed field-map contrast under concept resampling. It cannot identify a causal effect of training on other initializations or corpora. [Qwen2.5-Math, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.12122v1) · [Recorded checkpoint contrast](../results/robustness/compare.json).
- Sparse-autoencoder feature structure and co-occurrence accounts offer candidate explanations. The present feature summaries use the same examples to select and describe features. A mechanism or a ranking of methods would require different evidence. [Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.19750) · [Karkada et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15029) · [Saved feature summaries](../results/mvp/sae.json).
